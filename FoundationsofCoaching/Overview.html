Foundational Level · Learning Path

Foundations of Coaching

Estimated duration

2.5 hours over a two-week period

Build the fundamental coaching skills that help students reflect, take ownership, and identify meaningful next steps.

Learning Objectives

Throughout this learning path, you should gain these competencies.

Active Listening: Understand what active listening is and demonstrate effective listening.
Powerful Questions: Identify the difference between deeply powerful, moderately powerful, and not powerful questions.
Encouragement & Meaningful Feedback: Can provide appropriate encouragement and meaningful feedback from a coaching perspective.
Goal-Setting: Demonstrates ability to facilitate a meaningful goal-setting conversation.

What You'll Do

1

Workshop

Attend a live Foundations of Coaching workshop with a facilitator and peers.

2

Simulation

Complete an asynchronous Foundational Coaching Skills simulated scenario.

3

Self-Evaluation

Listen to your call recording to evaluate your new skills and reflect on your coaching.

Before You Begin

Required Assignment

If you have been added to this learning path, your leadership has assigned it as a required development activity.

Complete in Order

Complete each step before moving on to the next. Attend the workshop before beginning the simulation, and finish the simulation before starting the self-evaluation.

Prioritize Direct-Leader Tasks

Tasks assigned by your direct leader take priority over Development Program learning path activities. Plan your learning path work around those responsibilities.
