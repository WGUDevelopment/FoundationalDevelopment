Step Three

Self-Evaluation

Take time to reflect on your coaching by listening to a recording of one of your student appointments or live events. Identify where you demonstrated the foundational coaching skills and recognize opportunities to continue growing.

Completing Your Self-Evaluation

Follow the instructions below to complete this step of your Foundations of Coaching learning path.

1 Request Your Recording

After completing the Foundations of Coaching workshop and simulation, request a recording of a student appointment or live event. Use the recording request button below to notify the development team.

2 Review Your Recording

Once your recording is provided, listen carefully to your interaction. Pay particular attention to the four foundational coaching skills covered during the workshop and simulation.

3 Reflect on Your Coaching

Identify specific examples of effective coaching behaviors, moments you felt especially good about, and opportunities to approach future interactions differently. Consider the impact your coaching decisions had on the student.

4 Submit Your Self-Evaluation

Open the Foundations of Coaching Self-Evaluation quiz in this Canvas module. Answer the reflection questions using specific examples from your recording and submit your responses through Canvas.

Important Recording Requirement

The recording you evaluate must be from an interaction that occurred after you attended the Foundations of Coaching workshop and completed the Foundational Coaching Skills simulation. It may take a few days for the development supervisor to provide your recording. If you are a live event coach only, one of your live events will be recorded for you to review.

Request Your Recording

Ready to Begin Your Self-Evaluation?

Once you have completed the workshop and simulation, click the button below to request your recording.

You will be asked to enter your first and last name so the development team can identify your request.

What to Look for in Your Recording

As you listen to your recording, consider how effectively you demonstrated the following skills.

Active Listening

Were you fully present? Did you allow the student to finish their thoughts, acknowledge their experiences, and explore what they were sharing?

Powerful Questions

Did your questions encourage reflection, critical thinking, and independent discovery rather than simply directing the student toward an answer?

Encouragement & Meaningful Feedback

Did you recognize specific strengths, progress, or effort? Was your feedback encouraging, relevant, and actionable?

Goal-Setting

Did you help the student identify meaningful and realistic next steps? Did the student take ownership of their goals and action plan?

Submit Your Self-Evaluation
