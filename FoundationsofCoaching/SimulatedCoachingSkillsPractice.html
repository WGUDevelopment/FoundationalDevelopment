Step Two

Foundational Coaching Skills Simulation

Put your foundational coaching skills into practice through a simulated student interaction. You'll make coaching decisions, observe student reactions, and receive feedback as the conversation unfolds.

Before You Begin

You must attend the Foundations of Coaching workshop before beginning this simulation.

How It Works
  1. Enter your first and last name.
  2. Enter the passcode shared during the workshop on the previous step.
  3. Read each student response carefully.
  4. Select the response you would use as the peer navigator.
  5. Review the feedback and student reaction.
  6. Continue until the simulation is complete.
  7. Download your results and upload them to the Canvas simulation assignment on the next page.

If you don't remember the passcode, contact your workshop facilitator or another participant.
